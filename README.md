# Gikomba Market Stall Performance Analysis

**Author:** MARION ODUOR· PLP Data Analytics Cohort 2026

## Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For two weeks she recorded her daily sales and kept a rough record of stock for five products. She wants to know how her business is doing, whether it is growing, what she should restock and where she should focus her effort. This project answers those questions using core Python only, covering sales totals, week-on-week growth, strong and weak days, weekday versus weekend sales, low-stock alerts and product margins.

## How to Run

[Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/Reeescyberspacexx/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb), then select Runtime - Run all.

## Key Findings

1. **Sales are growing:** week 2 sales were KES 24,400 against KES 22,050 in week 1, which is 10.7% growth (total KES 46,450 over 14 days).
2. **Weekends are far stronger:** weekend days average KES 4,900 versus KES 2,685 on weekdays, which is 82.5% more per day.
3. **Two products need restocking now:** tomatoes (18 kg against a 25 kg reorder level) and sukuma (9 kg against 15 kg). Avocado has the best gross margin at 42.9%.
